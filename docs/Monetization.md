# Philosophy

Crown is monetized through **cosmetics, convenience, and coaching**, while keeping all competitive gameplay fair.

Except for interstitial advertising, this document describes post-MVP strategy. The canonical MVP boundary is [[Specs/MVP Scope Matrix]].

- No pay-to-win mechanics
- No gameplay advantages in rated matches
- Elo and League progression are determined solely by player skill
- Cosmetics are purely visual

---

# Free

## Gameplay

- Unlimited online play while connected and unlimited offline adaptive-bot play
- Full access to rated matches
- Full access to Leagues
- One Daily Puzzle
- Unlimited viewing of the player's latest 50 completed non-void match replays

## Advertising

- **Checkers:** Interstitial ad after every 3 matches
- **Chess:** Interstitial ad after every 2 matches

Only completed bot, rated matchmaking, and Elo-applying rated friend matches count. Unrated friend matches, rated friend results capped by the daily Elo limit, cancelled or void matches, and sessions without a committed result do not count. If an ad is unavailable, Crown continues without delaying the player and does not defer that ad.

# Future Seasonal Rewards

Players who meet the seasonal participation requirement (e.g. a minimum number of games played) earn an exclusive cosmetic item from the current seasonal collection.

---

# Future Cosmetic Collections

Collections are sold as one-time purchases and permanently owned.

A collection includes the following items:

- Avatar
- Board
- Frame
- Pieces
- Move animations
- Sound effects
- Badge
- Victory animation

Collections never affect gameplay.

## Collections Shop

Collections can be purchased through a Collections Shop screen within the Profile navigation tab. 

In a given season (initially planning for two a year - summer and winter), a promoted Collection will be available for purchase in its entirety. 

The Collections Shop will also present the user with two individual cosmetics for purchase from previous collections. These are refreshed weekly. 

The Collections Shop will also display a single cosmetic from the promoted collection, which users can earn by winning a certain number of matches within the season.

This system is inspired by the Night Market within Valorant.

# Crown Membership

Crown Membership is planned for a post-MVP release. The benefits below describe that future offering and are not shown as available during Alpha.

## Knight

**$0.99/month** or **$9.99/year**

Includes:

- No advertisements
- 20% discount on all cosmetic collections
- 3 AI match reviews per day
- One AI hint for the Daily Puzzle

---

## King

**$4.99/month** or **$49.99/year**

Includes everything in Knight, plus:

- Unlimited AI match reviews
- 30% discount on all cosmetic collections
- Access to every cosmetic collection while subscribed

Purchased collections remain permanently owned even if a membership expires.

---

# Daily Puzzle

- One new puzzle each day
- Does not affect Elo
- Does not affect League rankings
- Does not award currency in the MVP
- Hints are unavailable in the MVP
- When Membership launches, Knight and King members receive one AI-generated hint

---

# AI Coaching

## Match Review

AI-generated post-game analysis designed to help players improve by explaining mistakes, missed opportunities, and stronger alternatives.

Match review limits after Membership launches:

- Free: 1/day
- Knight: 3/day
- King: Unlimited

## Daily Puzzle Hint

Available to Knight and King members.

Provides a single AI-generated hint that nudges the player toward the correct idea without revealing the full solution.

The hint is generated during puzzle authoring, reviewed with the puzzle, and stored with the published puzzle. It is not generated on demand during play.

---

# Core Principles

- Competitive gameplay is never monetized.
- Cosmetics are the primary revenue source.
- Memberships provide convenience and learning tools.
- Permanent cosmetic ownership and subscriptions coexist:
  - Buy collections to own them forever.
  - Subscribe to King to access every collection while your membership is active.
- Future seasonal participation rewards may encourage regular play without affecting competitive balance.

The MVP provides only bundled board and piece selection. It has no shop, collection ownership, purchase, membership, seasonal reward, currency, or entitlement behavior.
